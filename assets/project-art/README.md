# Project artwork

Original concept illustrations generated with the built-in image-generation tool for this portfolio. These are explanatory visual metaphors, not screenshots, clinical evidence, or measured results. Generated PNGs were encoded as WebP at their original 1536 × 1024 dimensions for delivery on the website; composition was not changed.

- `careflow.webp`: administrative documents passing through an approval checkpoint.
- `evidence-ledger.webp`: source documents, a quotation, and a magnifying lens.

The exact workflow explanations are ordinary HTML beside the artwork. Motion is optional, respects the operating system’s reduced-motion preference, and can be paused using the footer control. Walkthroughs play only after the visitor presses Play.

## Generation prompts

### CareFlow

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original landscape project cover for a professional healthcare AI research portfolio, no title or labels. Create a premium editorial 3D paper-and-ceramic illustration explaining human-reviewed workflow automation by metaphor: a small orderly stack of administrative document cards on the left, a central navy checkpoint arch containing a tasteful burgundy approval seal with a check mark, and two neatly organized output document cards on the right. A slender curved path connects the composition, visually moving documents through review. Distinct, readily understandable objects, not an actual software screenshot and not a clinical system. Soft matte materials, elegant rounded geometry, crisp sculptural detail, natural studio shadows and a slightly elevated three-quarter camera. Wide landscape composition about 3:2, main objects in the central 70%, generous breathing room on all sides for responsive website cropping. Seamless warm ivory background #f5f3ee; midnight navy #1b3040, muted burgundy #813f4e, pale blue-gray #d9e3e7, restrained sage #9cafa3. Sophisticated, quiet, visually engaging, not childish. No humans, no robots, no medical equipment, no patient data, no chart statistics, no letters, no words, no logo, no watermark.

### Evidence Ledger

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original landscape project cover for a professional healthcare AI research portfolio, no title or labels. Create a premium editorial 3D paper-and-ceramic illustration explaining tracing an answer back to its source: a beautifully crafted oversized magnifying lens with a midnight navy rim and muted burgundy handle floats just above an open cream reference book; beneath the lens a short quotation strip on a document is highlighted in pale sage. Two simple document cards sit behind the book, connected to the quoted strip by fine curved burgundy threads. Distinct, readily understandable objects. Metaphor for source inspection, not an actual software screenshot and not a clinical evidence claim. Soft matte materials, elegant rounded geometry, crisp sculptural detail, natural studio shadows and a slightly elevated three-quarter camera. Wide landscape composition about 3:2, main objects in the central 70%, generous breathing room on all sides for responsive website cropping. Seamless warm ivory background #f5f3ee; midnight navy #1b3040, muted burgundy #813f4e, pale blue-gray #d9e3e7, restrained sage #9cafa3. Match a sophisticated, quiet, contemporary editorial illustration family, not childish. No humans, robots, medical equipment, patient data, fake graphs, readable text, logos, or watermark.
